■ 0. 개념 30초
- 버전(1.0.1) = 사용자에게 보이는 번호. 빌드(15) = 그 버전의 몇 번째 파일인지.
  한 버전에 빌드는 여러 개 만들 수 있고, 스토어엔 그중 하나만 붙임 → 우리는 15.
- TestFlight = 심사 전에 우리끼리 설치해보는 곳. App Store 심사와는 별개.
- 제출 = "이 빌드로 1.0.1 심사해 주세요". 보통 24시간 안에 결과.
- 수동 게시 = 승인돼도 내가 "Release" 버튼을 누르기 전엔 스토어에 안 올라감(안전장치).
- OTA = 앱 안의 JS만 바꾸는 업데이트. 심사 없이 바로 나가고, 앱은 다음 실행 때 적용.
  (하자 섹션 개선·사진 그리드는 OTA로 나감 → 심사관도 같은 걸 봄)

2) OTA가 발행됐다고 들으면: 앱 완전종료 → 실행 → 5초 → 완전종료 → 실행 (2회)
3) 확인: 카메라 연속 촬영 · 채팅 읽으면 배지 사라짐 · 업데이트 팝업 1회 · 햅틱 ·
   하자 토글/사진/없음/자동체크 · 사진 3열 그리드+순서 정하기

1.0.1 is a visual/interaction update (sheets, haptics, avatar crop, country picker, photo ordering, camera capture, activity bell, condition/flaws fields, in-app update notice); no new data collection.

GUEST ACCESS: No sign-in is required to browse. After the language screen, the welcome screen offers a prominent "Browse as guest" button that opens the public feed — browsing, search, listing details, and seller profiles all work without an account. SIGN-IN (only for account features — favoriting, chat, posting, offers, reviews, reporting): tap any of those and a sign-in prompt appears; use "Continue with Apple" — the reviewer's own Apple ID creates an account immediately, no demo credentials needed. Email one-time-code login also exists as an alternative. The marketplace is pre-seeded with sample listings.

DEVICES TESTED: iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.6 — physical device (TestFlight builds 3–8), full end-to-end testing including Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, push notifications, and location features. iOS Simulator (Xcode 16): iPhone 16 Pro Max and additional screen sizes — a 46-step end-to-end UI test pass in English, Korean, and Chinese.

APP AND AUDIENCE: Torrens Market is a free local secondhand marketplace for Adelaide, South Australia. Users (16+) list used items with photos, browse and search nearby listings, chat, agree on a price, and meet in person to complete the trade. Fully trilingual (English/Korean/Chinese) for Adelaide's international community, with local-trust features (suburb verification, verified-seller badge, post-trade reviews). The app processes no payments; trades settle in person. No paid content, purchases, or subscriptions; no App Tracking Transparency prompt (no tracking).

MAIN FEATURES: Home feed (browse/filters), search tab, + button (post a listing), chat tab (price offers + meetup scheduling), Profile > Settings (language, blocked users, delete account). UGC safety: every listing has Report (reason picker) and Block seller; three distinct reports auto-hide a listing. Account deletion: Profile > Settings > Delete account (type DELETE to confirm).

EXTERNAL SERVICES: Supabase (hosted) — authentication, Postgres database, file storage, edge functions (entire backend). Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In — federated login only. Expo / EAS (expo.dev) — build pipeline, over-the-air JS updates, Expo Push Notification service (APNs). GitHub Pages — static hosting for the privacy policy and support pages. No payment processors, no ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no AI services.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: None — the app functions identically in all regions. UI language (EN/KO/ZH) is a user choice, independent of region. Listings are naturally concentrated in Adelaide, Australia, where the service launches.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: Not applicable. Consumer-to-consumer secondhand marketplace; no payments processed, no financial/medical/gambling services, no protected third-party material. All content is user-generated with reporting, blocking, and auto-hide moderation built in.
