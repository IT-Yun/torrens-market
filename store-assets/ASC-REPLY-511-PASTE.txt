Hello, thank you for the detailed review.

We have resolved the Guideline 5.1.1(v) issue in the newly attached build 1.0.0 (8).

WHAT CHANGED
The app no longer requires registration to access for-sale listings. On first launch, after choosing a language, the welcome screen offers Sign in with Apple, Google, or email — and a prominent "Browse as guest" button. Tapping it opens the public home feed with no account. As a guest, users can:
- browse the home feed and filter by category and distance,
- search listings with keywords and filters,
- open any listing's detail page (photos, price, description, item details, approximate location),
- view a seller's public profile, trust tier, and reviews.

Registration is now requested only for account-based features, at the moment the user invokes them: favoriting a listing, chatting with a seller, posting a listing, making a price offer, scheduling a meetup, leaving a review, and reporting or blocking. Tapping any of these as a guest shows a sign-in prompt (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email one-time code) and returns the user to where they left off after signing in.

HOW TO VERIFY
Launch build 8 fresh: after the language screen, tap "Browse as guest" on the welcome screen. The marketplace feed appears with no login step; browse, search, and open listings freely. Tap the heart on any listing (or the Chat button on a listing page) to see the sign-in sheet appear only at that point — it offers Apple, Google, or email sign-in and can be dismissed to keep browsing.

A short screen recording is attached demonstrating exactly this on a physical iPhone 17 Pro: fresh launch → language selection → "Browse as guest" → browsing the feed and a listing detail with no sign-in → tapping the heart → the sign-in sheet appearing only then.

All information previously provided in this thread (screen recording, tested devices, app description, access instructions, external services, regional notes) still applies to build 8; the App Review Information Notes have been updated to describe guest browsing.
