① Promotional Text (170자 이내)

Adelaide's local secondhand marketplace — in English, Korean and Chinese. Browse without an account, sell in a minute, meet safely. Free.

② Description (기존 내용 전체 삭제 후 붙여넣기)
----------------------------------------
Torrens Market is Adelaide's secondhand marketplace, built for the city's international communities — with the entire app available in English, Korean and Chinese.

BROWSE FIRST, SIGN IN LATER
• Open the app and see what's for sale nearby — no account needed to look around
• Sign in with Apple, Google or email only when you want to favourite, chat or sell

BUY & SELL LOCALLY
• Browse by distance: your suburb, within 5–20 km, or all Adelaide
• 16 categories with the details that matter: furniture dimensions, cosmetics expiry, car rego and service history — skip anything you're unsure about
• Post in under a minute: shoot photos with the camera or pick from your library, choose the cover, arrange the order
• Honest listings: a clear condition scale plus an optional "has flaws" section with its own photos and notes

TRUST YOU CAN SEE
• Post-trade reviews grow your Aussie-animal trust tier: quokka → bilby → koala → wombat → wallaby → kangaroo
• Verified-suburb badges and a verified-only filter
• Report and block anywhere; repeatedly reported listings are hidden automatically

DEAL-MAKING BUILT IN
• 1:1 chat with photos, price offers and pickup meetups — accepting a meetup reserves the item
• An activity bell that gathers chats, offers, meetups, reviews and favourites on your listings
• Keyword alerts: get notified the moment something you want is posted

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Locations are rounded to about 1 km on your device — your exact position never leaves your phone
• No ads, no tracking, free to use
• Delete your account and all data anytime

Made in Adelaide, for Adelaide. Named after the River Torrens.

③ What's New in This Version
----------------------------------------
A smoother, more polished Torrens Market:
- Redesigned pop-ups and sheets — swipe to dismiss, with subtle haptics
- Circular profile-photo cropping that matches how your avatar is shown
- Pick your nationality from any country — search by name, flag shown automatically
- Faster, smoother scrolling on the home feed
- Consistent spacing, shadows and motion across the whole app
- Choose a cover photo and set the photo order when you post a listing
- Pickup and payment conditions (e.g. pickup only, cash only) now shown on every listing in the feed
- Listing page shows the description first, then item details
- In chat, tapping the other person's name opens their profile
- A gentle notice when a new version is available, shown once per update
- Activity bell: see chats, offers, meetups, reviews and favorites on your listings in one place
- Take photos with the camera while posting — shoot, use, shoot again
- Clearer item condition (4 levels) plus an optional "has flaws" section with its own photos and note
- Skip fields you're unsure about — only the essentials are required
- Small fixes and performance improvements

• 광고 없음, 추적 없음, 무료
• 언제든 계정과 데이터 삭제 가능

Torrens 市集是阿德莱德的本地二手交易应用，专为这座城市的留学生与移民社区打造，整个应用支持中文、英文和韩文。

先逛逛，再登录
• 打开应用就能看到附近在售的物品 —— 浏览无需账号
• 只有在收藏、聊天或出售时才需要用 Apple、Google 或邮箱登录

本地买卖
• 按距离浏览：我的区域、5–20 公里内或全阿德莱德
• 16 个分类，只保留真正有用的细节：家具尺寸、化妆品有效期、汽车 rego 与保养记录 —— 不确定的字段可留空
• 一分钟发布：用相机直接拍或从相册选择，设置封面并排序
• 诚信发布：清晰的成色档位，以及可选的"有瑕疵"栏目（瑕疵照片与说明）

看得见的信任
• 交易后评价累积澳洲动物信用等级：短尾矮袋鼠 → 兔耳袋狸 → 考拉 → 袋熊 → 沙袋鼠 → 袋鼠
• 区域认证徽章，可只看认证卖家
• 随处可举报和屏蔽，多次被举报的商品自动隐藏

聊天里完成交易
• 一对一聊天、图片、出价与面交约定 —— 接受约定即为商品预留
• 活动铃铛汇总你商品收到的聊天、出价、约定、评价与收藏
• 关键词提醒：心仪物品一上架立刻推送

隐私优先
• 位置在手机上按约 1 公里取整 —— 精确位置绝不上传
• 无广告、无跟踪、完全免费
• 随时可删除账号及全部数据

阿德莱德制造，为阿德莱德而生。以托伦斯河命名。
