Torrens Market is a free local marketplace for buying and selling secondhand items in Adelaide, South Australia — built for the city's international community, with the entire app available in English, Korean and Chinese.

BROWSE WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
See what's for sale near you the moment you open the app. Filter by category and distance, search by keyword, and open any listing — no sign-up needed to look around.

SELL IN A MINUTE
Photos, a title, a price and your suburb. Category-specific fields (furniture dimensions, cosmetics expiry, car details) help buyers trust what they see. Bump your listing once a day.

DEAL SAFELY
Chat in-app, make or accept price offers, and schedule a pickup — busy public places recommended. Cash or bank transfer, settled in person; Torrens Market never handles your money.

TRUST YOU CAN SEE
Post-trade reviews grow your Aussie animal trust tier (Quokka → Kangaroo). Suburb verification and verified-seller badges. Report and block anyone, anytime.

KEYWORD ALERTS
Tell us what you're looking for and get a push notification the moment it's posted.

PRIVACY FIRST
Only your approximate location (~1 km) is ever stored. No ads, no tracking.

Trilingual · Adelaide only · Free.
