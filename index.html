What is Torrens Market?

A free secondhand trading app built for the people of Adelaide — with a fully trilingual interface (English, 한국어, 中文), distance-based browsing, verified suburbs, and safety features designed in from day one.

Why it's different

  • Your language, natively — the entire app switches between English, Korean, and Chinese.
  • Trade within your community — optional nationality on profiles with an opt-in filter.
  • Details that matter — category-specific fields: furniture dimensions, cosmetics expiry dates, car rego & service history.
  • Trust you can see — post-trade reviews build an Aussie-animal trust ladder (quokka → kangaroo), plus verified-suburb and phone badges.
  • Privacy by design — locations are rounded to ~1 km on your device; exact GPS never leaves your phone. No ads, no tracking.
